about=Point the plugin at a directory and it finds Georgian-named data and renames it to clean, filesystem- and SQL-safe Latin using the Georgian national romanization (lowercase, digraphs sh/ch/ts/gh/kh, no apostrophes); any other symbol becomes an underscore, with automatic collision handling (_2, _3). It renames: shapefile filesets (all sidecars together), database container files (.gpkg/.sqlite/.gdb), and — via GDAL/OGR — the layers/feature classes and field names inside GeoPackage, SQLite/Spatialite and Esri FileGDB. Always preview the old→new plan first; then apply in place, or work on a copy so the original is left untouched. Bilingual ka/en. Free/open-source, GPLv3.

    0.1.0 - First release. Directory scan; shapefiles (+sidecars), gpkg/sqlite/gdb files, and their layers/feature classes and field names; Georgian national transliteration with symbol→"_" and collision handling; preview table; in-place or copy mode; bilingual ka/en.
